Lugia

Lugia came from Pokémon the Movie 2000: The Power of One, and from the video games Pokémon Gold, Pokémon Silver, and Pokémon crystal. I fell in love with Lugia because of the movie. When I was a child, I was a huge fan of the Pokémon TV show, and my family couldn't afford to get the games or the consoles. So it started from the Show. I watched Pokémon the First Movie, and loved it. But something about the movie 2000 hit completely different. The majestic deep voice they chose to voice Lugia (voice actor Eric Rath), the inclusion of the legendary birds (Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres), and the most impactful, the beautiful song played several times throughout the movie. The song is partially used to be Lugia's cry. Like a whale song, he sings this tune. The apex of the song is the ocarina and instrumental form of the beautiful song at the end of the movie. It was by far the most impactful part of the movie to me and felt like the most beautiful thing I'd ever heard in my young life at that moment. It solidified my love for the movie and my love for this pokemon.

Lugia's Pokemon Profile

As of Pokémon generation 9, Lugia is a Psychic/Flying type Pokémon. Lugia is considered a legendary Pokémon, and is the box art legendary (meaning it is featured on the game package) for both Pokémon Silver and Pokémon Soul Silver. Lugia has the following base stats:

  • HP: 106 — Good stat
  • Attack: 90 — Above Average stat
  • Defense: 130 — Very Good stat
  • Sp. Attack: 90 — Above Average stat
  • Sp. Defense: 154 — Excellent stat
  • Speed: 110 — Good stat

Favorite Lugia Cards

This card holds special significance to me because I remember having this card as a child. The art is a very lovely artistic style that, to me, resembles Starry Night from Vincent Van Gogh.

This card is unlike any I'd ever seen before. The image seemingly breaking the card, the intensity of the art, and the water's colors are incredible.

This card is one of the reasons why people really love full art cards. The artistry, the ominous feeling, the background featuring a person witnessing a creature significantly bigger than him, it's all something that makes these cards amazing to see and collect.
