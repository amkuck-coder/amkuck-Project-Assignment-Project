Sylveon

Sylveon was an unexpected addition to the games Pokémon X and Pokémon Y. When the games were first announced, the Pokémon types were already determined. These games were generation 6, and there hadn't been a new Pokémon type added since generation 2 which was ~14 years prior to the release of generation 6. When generation 6 was announced, so was the new type, Fairy type, and a new Eevee evolution, Sylveon. As a massive fan of pokemon who loved the first 3 generations, didn't care for the 4th generation, and did not like the 5th generation, I was extremely skeptical of the new type and whether I would hate it or not. Because of this, I felt it appropriate to obtain the new Pokémon, Sylveon, to test out the type and see how I feel. Sylveon, Fairy type, and Pokémon X and Y in general led me to falling back in love with the franchise as a whole. Eevee evolves into Sylveon with love, making it also one of the most affectionate concepts for a Pokémon. It also, in my opinion, has one of the best shiny variants of any Pokémon.

Sylveon - Pokemon Sleep

Sylveon's Pokemon Profile

As of Pokémon generation 9, Sylveon is a Fairy type pokemon. Sylveon evolves with love from an Eevee and is the final evolution in its line. It has been made available in every single main line game ever since its debut in generation 6. The only remake or major side game that Sylveon is NOT obtainable in after generation 6 is Pokémon let's Go, Pikachu! or Pokémon Let's Go, Eevee!. Sylveon has the following base stats:

  • HP: 95 — Good
  • Attack: 65 — Average
  • Defense: 65 — Average
  • Sp. Attack: 110 — Good
  • Sp. Defense: 130 — Very Good
  • Speed: 60 — Average
